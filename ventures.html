WealthHorizon by Muneris

Own your whole financial picture.

A self-hosted wealth and tax platform for multi-jurisdiction portfolios that the mainstream budgeting apps cannot handle.

The problem

Investors with genuinely complex affairs are stuck with tools designed for budgeting. Alternative assets — angel investments, crowdfunding, private holdings and crypto — simply do not exist in the mainstream apps. Aggregators require you to hand your entire financial life to a third-party cloud before they will work at all. And almost none of them do proper capital-gains matching, which is the part that actually matters when the portfolio is complicated.

Planning tools make it worse by drawing a straight line into the future. A deterministic projection ignores sequence-of-returns risk, and net worth on its own never answers the only question that counts: will this hold up?

The approach

WealthHorizon combines four things nothing else puts in one place: data sovereignty, genuine multi-asset and multi-jurisdiction coverage, real tax-lot accounting, and probabilistic planning.

What it does

  • Data sovereignty — runs on your own hardware; nobody else holds the data.
  • Every asset, every border — pensions, ISAs, angel, crowdfunding and crypto, across jurisdictions and currencies.
  • FIFO tax-lot engine — real capital-gains accounting by jurisdiction and tax year, with export-ready packs.
  • Monte Carlo planning — plan-success probability rather than a single hopeful line.
  • Net-worth dashboard — every account, jurisdiction and currency in one view.

Built and working, not a wireframe: PostgreSQL, Node/TypeScript and React/Vite, Dockerised and self-hostable on a NAS.
