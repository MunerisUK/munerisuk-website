About

Senior hands, brought in when it matters.

Muneris is an independent consulting practice providing interim leadership and advisory support to organisations in growth, change or transition. Work spans professional services, IT and digital delivery, and product and software assessment — across both the private and public sectors.

The practice

Board-level governance, with hands still on the delivery.

Every engagement is led personally and scoped to a clear outcome — whether that is stepping into a leadership seat, reviewing a service, or giving investors a straight read on maturity and risk.

The combination is deliberately unusual: investor-grade financial acuity and board-level governance on one side, and hands-on delivery of complex change at global scale on the other. That has meant initiating a $1.5B strategic acquisition, delivering more than £8M of annualised cost-out — roughly 25% — while improving throughput and customer outcomes, and scaling AI, cloud and cyber across the UK, US and Asia.

The work happens comfortably alongside private equity sponsors, public-sector executives and regulators. It usually involves building high-performing multi-country teams, and reliably turning around functions and programmes that were not going to land on their own.

Muneris — from the Latin munus: service, duty, and the trust placed in someone who serves.

Experience

Stabilised a critical Electronic Patient Record, reducing operational risk and enabling sustainable clinical adoption. Established and chairs Digital Health Advisory and AI Governance.

  • 2023 – 2024

    NHS Kent & Medway ICB — Executive Director, Digital Transformation
