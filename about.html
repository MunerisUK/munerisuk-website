Martin Carpenter

FBCS · FIoD · CHCIO — technology and transformation leader with more than twenty years creating enterprise value across healthcare and technology.

Profile

Board-level governance, with hands still on the delivery.

Muneris Management Ltd is the practice through which Martin Carpenter takes on advisory, non-executive and interim leadership work.

The combination is deliberately unusual: investor-grade financial acuity and board-level governance on one side, and hands-on delivery of complex change at global scale on the other. That means initiating a $1.5B strategic acquisition, delivering more than £8M of annualised cost-out — roughly 25% — while improving throughput and customer outcomes, and scaling AI, cloud and cyber across the UK, US and Asia.

The work happens comfortably alongside private equity sponsors, public-sector executives and regulators. It usually involves building high-performing multi-country teams, and reliably turning around programmes that were not going to land on their own.

Career

Twenty years, one through-line.

  • 2024 – present

    Government of Jersey, Health & Community Services — Healthcare CIO

    Designed and gained ministerial approval for phase one of a five-year £40M digital transformation to improve access, workforce efficiency and outcomes. Stabilised a critical Electronic Patient Record, reducing operational risk and enabling sustainable clinical adoption. Established and chairs Digital Health Advisory and AI Governance.

  • 2023 – 2024

    NHS Kent & Medway ICB — Executive Director, Digital Transformation

    Executive lead for digital, data and technology in a £4B opex system serving 2M+ residents, achieving a 20% in-year cost reduction. Built innovation partnerships and multi-organisation research data environments, and was Senior Responsible Owner for a federated research digital platform.

  • 2022

    Calyx.AI — Interim VP, Solution Deployment

    Directed a 400+ person global professional services organisation to recover clinical trial delivery performance, generating more than $1M of savings in six months through cloud automation and process redesign.

  • 2021 – 2022

    Synomics.AI — Chief Information Officer

    Delivered a market-ready combinatorial genomics product in six months and cut genomic processing from about a week to roughly 24 hours. Stabilised budget through AWS cost optimisation and instituted product cadence and governance to scale from prototype to paying customers.

  • 2020 – 2021

    ColorTokens (US) — Consulting CISO / CIO

    Originated and delivered zero-trust cybersecurity solutions for public and commercial healthcare clients, including national provider organisations.

  • 2016 – 2020

    Optum UK (UnitedHealth Group) — Chief Information Officer

    Owned UK technology strategy for a $240B US healthcare insurer with board-level governance. Reduced technology spend by £8M (~25%), delivered digital products to around half of NHS GP practices handling ~20M clinical transactions a month, and initiated the $1.5B EMIS acquisition. Achieved Cyber Essentials Plus and implemented ISO 27001 / 20000.

  • 2014 – 2016

    Reporting Online LLP — IT Director

    24×7 diagnostic teleradiology provider to 65 NHS acute trusts. Created a board-approved technology strategy, delivered a seamless migration of 24/7 services and reduced KPI breaches through process, organisation and systems change.
