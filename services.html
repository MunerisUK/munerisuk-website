Services

Senior technology capability, on the terms you need it.

Four practices, one operator. Engagements are scoped to a decision or an outcome — not to a headcount or a retainer that never ends.

01 — Board & non-executive advisory

Independent challenge the board can act on.

Boards rarely lack technology opinions. What they lack is someone who can tell them which of those opinions is load-bearing, what it will cost, and what happens if the answer is deferred another year.

Muneris provides non-executive and advisory input on digital, data and AI: prioritising investment, setting the governance that keeps regulators comfortable, and giving executives an honest second opinion before the money is committed.

Typically engaged for

  • Non-executive director and board advisor roles
  • Digital, data and AI investment prioritisation
  • AI governance and assurance frameworks
  • Board reporting that survives scrutiny
  • Independent review of major technology business cases
02 — Interim & fractional leadership

Cover that leaves the organisation stronger than it found it.

Group CIO, CTO, Chief Digital & AI Officer or Chief Transformation Officer — full interim mandates or a fractional arrangement where a permanent hire cannot yet be justified.

The pattern is consistent: stabilise what is failing, rebuild confidence with the executive and the workforce, put an operating model in place, and hand over to a permanent leader who inherits something that works.

Typically engaged for

  • Interim CIO / CTO / CDIO mandates
  • Turnaround of challenged programmes and EPR/ERP estates
  • Operating model and organisation design
  • Global and multi-country delivery teams
  • Vendor, outsourcing and commercial renegotiation
03 — Private equity value creation

Diligence, integration and cost-out, in sponsor language.

Technology is usually where the thesis is either proved or quietly lost. Muneris works with sponsors and portfolio company boards as an operating partner on technology — before the deal and long after it.

That includes originating and running strategic and technical diligence, building the hundred-day plan, integrating acquisitions, and taking real cost out of the run-rate without stalling the roadmap.

Typically engaged for

  • Strategic and technical due diligence
  • M&A integration and carve-out planning
  • Value-creation planning and 100-day execution
  • Cost-out and productivity programmes
  • Operating partner (technology) support to boards
04 — Data, AI & cyber

Built for environments where the regulator is a stakeholder.

Cloud and data platforms, AI adoption and governance, and zero-trust security — designed and delivered in UK and US regulated settings where getting it wrong is a reportable event, not a retrospective item.

Practical experience spans AWS and Azure platform builds, federated research data environments, ISO 27001 and ISO 20000 implementation, GDPR compliance, Cyber Essentials Plus and zero-trust architecture.

Typically engaged for

  • Data platform and analytics strategy
  • AI governance, assurance and safe adoption
  • Cloud migration, optimisation and FinOps
  • Zero-trust and cybersecurity programmes
  • ISO 27001 / ISO 20000 / GDPR readiness
